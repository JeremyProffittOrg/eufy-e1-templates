Eufy E1 UV hold-down templates — Draft 1

Eight plates. Mini bed is 318×86 mm (fits the 330×90 printable area and an H2D). Standard bed is a 308×308 mm centre plate (318 trimmed 10 mm in X and Y so it fits the H2D).
